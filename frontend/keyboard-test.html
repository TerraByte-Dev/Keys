keyboard.js — 88 keys, A0 to C8

Click and drag across the keys to glissando. Velocity comes from how low on the key you press.
ES modules will not load over file://. Serve this directory first, e.g. .venv\Scripts\python.exe -m http.server 8080 from the repo root, then open http://localhost:8080/frontend/keyboard-test.html.
